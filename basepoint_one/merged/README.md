# The Reciprocal Xi Function at the Basepoint One and Across the Critical Strip

Nick Polson and Vadim Sokolov, September 2026. One merged paper (45 pages) combining five notes:
the basepoint one note, the scale mixture note, the process map, the Sato and Bondesson note, and the eta analogue.

basepoint_one_merged.tex / .pdf   the paper; compile with pdflatex three times (figures fig_*.pdf in this folder)

Contents
  1  Introduction
  2  Setting and the Mellin side (Williams, Biane Pitman Yor, size biasing)
  3  The Thorin measure and the law of X_1
  4  The sine squared lemma and the Levy form of xi(1+s)
  5  Below the basepoint one (zero free half planes, explicit error formula over the zeros)
  6  Hitting times, Kent's theorem and Brownian boundaries (alpha = 1/2, 0.6, 0.75, 1)
  7  The reciprocal on the real axis: normal scale mixtures and extended GGCs in the strip
  8  Infinite divisibility: what Sato and Bondesson give
  9  Completeness: Wiener, Salem and Riesz
  10 A map of stochastic processes (Wiener gamma, Riemann OU, subordinated BM, the map)
  11 The Dedekind eta analogue L(s, chi_12)
  12 Conclusion
